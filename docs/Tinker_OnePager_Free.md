# TINKER — One-Page Concept

## The Product in One Sentence
A free, open-source project-embedded learning notebook for makers that turns failure into data and requires proof of skill.

## The Problem
Builders learn by doing, but every note-taking app treats learning and making as separate activities. Notes live in Notion. Projects live in the garage. The gap between them is where real learning dies.

## The Solution
Tinker is a digital workshop. Every project is a workbench. Every note is nailed to a specific build. Every failure is tagged and tracked. Every skill must be proven with evidence.

## Three Things That Make It Different

1. **Failure Archaeology** — You don't just document what worked. You categorize what failed, why, and at what cost. Over time, you see your personal failure patterns.

2. **Evidence-Based Mastery** — You cannot mark a skill "learned" without uploading a photo, video, or file proving you can do it. A 30-day challenge forces you to prove it again.

3. **Human-Drawn Bridges** — When you see a connection between your woodworking and your coding, you draw it yourself. The tool preserves it. No AI suggestions. Your analogies are yours.

## Who It's For
Self-taught polymath makers: weekend woodworkers, indie hackers, DIY biologists, creative technologists — anyone who learns with their hands across multiple domains.

## What It Looks Like
An infinite canvas of workbenches. Zoom out to see your whole workshop. Zoom in to work on a single project. Pan between shops. Everything is spatial, physical, and persistent.

## Free Forever
- No subscriptions, no paywalls, no feature gates
- Open source (MIT License)
- Local-first: your data lives on your device
- No accounts, no tracking, no data collection
- Funded by voluntary donations and grants, not by users
